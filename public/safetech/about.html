SafeTech Solutions builds workplace health and safety technology designed to make training accessible, measurable and genuinely useful for operational teams.

What We Do

Purpose-built for workplace safety

We focus on one thing: making health and safety training work better for the people who need it most.

01 · Mission

Help organisations deliver effective H&S training

Our mission is to give workers, supervisors and administrators the tools they need to run consistent, auditable health and safety training — without making it complicated or impractical for busy operational environments.

02 · Approach

Practical learning grounded in real decisions

We combine short learning content with scenario-based workplace decisions, knowledge assessments, and clear progress records. Every element of the training experience is designed to reinforce safer habits rather than tick compliance boxes.

03 · Values

Clarity, accessibility and genuine usefulness

We build systems that are clear for workers to use, useful for supervisors to monitor, and straightforward for administrators to manage. Accessibility and practical application guide every design and development decision we make.

Our Platform

SafeWork: our H&S training platform

SafeWork is a browser-based health and safety training platform for warehouse, logistics and operational teams. Workers complete interactive training modules on any device, supervisors monitor real-time team progress, and administrators control content and user management from a single dashboard.

  • Interactive scenario-based learning for each module
  • Configurable knowledge assessments with instant feedback
  • Pass/fail scoring with configurable pass marks (default 70%)
  • Certificate generation and download on completion
  • Supervisor training assignment and monitoring tools
  • Administrator controls for users, modules and questions
  • CSV export for compliance reporting

Training Content

Five core health & safety modules

SafeWork includes five training modules covering the essential health and safety topics for warehouse and logistics environments.

💪

Manual Handling

Safe lifting, posture, risk assessment and team lift procedures.

⚠️

Hazard Awareness

Identifying and responding to slips, trips, blocked exits and vehicle routes.

🦺

PPE Awareness

Selecting, inspecting and correctly wearing personal protective equipment.

🔥

Fire Safety

Fire risk, alarm procedures, evacuation routes and assembly points.

🚛

Moving Vehicles & Equipment

Safe working near forklifts, blind spots, pedestrian routes and authorisation.
