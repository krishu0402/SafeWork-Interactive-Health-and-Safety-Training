About the Platform

What is SafeWork?

SafeWork is a browser-based health and safety training platform designed specifically for warehouse and logistics teams. Workers complete structured training modules at their own pace — on any device, without needing to download anything or attend a classroom session.

Each module combines short learning content, a realistic workplace scenario, and a timed knowledge assessment. When a worker passes, a verifiable certificate is generated automatically. Supervisors and administrators can track every step in real time.

  • 100% browser-based — no app or installation required
  • Workers learn at their own pace on any device
  • Covers the most common warehouse and logistics H&S risks
  • Three distinct user roles: Worker, Supervisor, Administrator
  • Instant certificate generation on module completion
  • Full audit trail for compliance and reporting

Training Modules

Covering the essentials of workplace safety

SafeWork includes five core health and safety training modules, each with focused learning content, an interactive workplace scenario and a timed knowledge assessment.

💪

Manual Handling

Safe lifting techniques, risk assessment, correct posture and team lift procedures to prevent musculoskeletal injuries on the warehouse floor.

⚠️

Hazard Awareness

Identifying and responding to workplace hazards including slips, trips, blocked emergency exits, spills and vehicle-pedestrian conflict zones.

🦺

PPE Awareness

Selecting, inspecting and correctly wearing personal protective equipment for different workplace roles, tasks and environmental conditions.

🔥

Fire Safety

Fire risk recognition, alarm response procedures, safe evacuation routes, assembly point locations and appropriate use of fire extinguishers.

🚛

Moving Vehicles & Equipment

Safety rules for working near forklifts and other moving vehicles — blind spots, designated pedestrian routes and authorisation requirements.

Learning Journey

How learning works

Every SafeWork module follows a consistent three-stage learning journey — designed to build genuine understanding, not just tick-box compliance.

Stage 01 — Content Slides

📖 Content Slides

Each module opens with a set of concise learning slides covering the key health and safety concepts for the topic. Workers read through the material at their own pace before moving on to the scenario stage. The content is written in plain, accessible language designed for operational workers — no jargon, no unnecessary length.

Stage 02 — Scenario

🎭 Realistic Workplace Scenario

After completing the content slides, workers face a realistic workplace decision scenario drawn from the module topic. They must read the situation and choose the correct course of action from a set of options. The scenario is designed to reflect genuine situations they may encounter in a warehouse or logistics environment, reinforcing the learning in a practical context.

Stage 03 — Assessment

🎯 Timed Knowledge Assessment

The final stage is a timed quiz covering the key learning points from the module. Workers answer multiple-choice questions against a countdown timer. On submission, scores are calculated and displayed instantly. Workers who meet or exceed the pass mark receive their certificate immediately. Those who fall short can retake the module after review.

User Roles

Platform features by role

SafeWork uses a three-tier role structure so that every user — whether a warehouse operative, a line supervisor or a site administrator — sees only the tools and data relevant to them.

👷 Worker
  • Complete assigned training modules
  • Work through content slides, scenarios and assessments
  • View personal training history and attempt records
  • Download and print earned certificates
  • Retake modules after a failed attempt
  • Manage personal profile and account details
📋 Supervisor
  • View real-time team training dashboard
  • Assign training modules to individual workers
  • Set due dates for assigned training
  • View detailed progress reports per worker
  • Monitor failed assessments and flag for follow-up
  • Download team training summary reports
🔧 Administrator
  • Create, edit and manage user accounts
  • Assign and update user roles across the organisation
  • Manage training modules and module content
  • Create and edit assessment questions
  • Configure pass marks and assessment settings
  • Access organisation-wide compliance reports and certificates
Certification

Verifiable training certificates

When a worker successfully passes a module assessment, SafeWork automatically generates a training certificate. Each certificate is uniquely identifiable and contains all the information required for compliance purposes.

  • Unique certificate reference code for every completion
  • Worker name displayed on the certificate
  • Module name and training area clearly stated
  • Assessment score and pass/fail status recorded
  • Date of issue for accurate record-keeping
  • Printable format suitable for personnel files

Certificates are accessible at any time from the worker's profile and from the administrator's compliance reporting area.
