HOW IT WORKS

Learn. Practise. Work safely.

Follow a clear journey from your workplace account to completed training, useful feedback and visible progress.

01

Sign in securely

Use your workplace account to access the area assigned to your role. Your credentials remain private and should never be shared.

02

Open your training

Workers see assigned modules and due dates. Select a module to review its learning content and begin the assessment.

03

Practise through scenarios

Use realistic workplace examples to think through safe choices, hazards, protective equipment and emergency actions.

04

Complete the assessment

Answer knowledge-check questions. Your score and completion status are recorded against the relevant assignment.

05

Review your progress

Workers can review results and certificates. Supervisors and administrators can review team activity and reports.

WORKER JOURNEY

Learn at your pace

Open assigned training, complete assessments and use feedback to improve understanding.

SUPERVISOR JOURNEY

Keep the team moving

Review worker status, assign modules and identify learning that needs attention.

ADMIN JOURNEY

Maintain oversight

Manage users and training modules while reviewing platform-level activity and reports.
