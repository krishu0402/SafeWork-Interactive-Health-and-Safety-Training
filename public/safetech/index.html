Our Flagship Product

SafeWork — H&S Training That Works

SafeWork is a browser-based workplace health and safety training platform designed for operational and logistics environments. Workers complete interactive modules on their own device, supervisors monitor team progress in real time, and administrators manage content and compliance from a single dashboard.

  • Interactive scenarios for each training module
  • Timed knowledge assessments with instant feedback
  • Pass/fail scoring with configurable pass marks
  • Certificate generation and download on completion
  • Supervisor training assignment and reporting tools
  • Administrator controls for users, modules and questions

Training Modules

Covering the essentials of workplace safety

SafeWork includes five core health and safety training modules, each with interactive scenarios and knowledge assessments.

💪

Manual Handling

Safe lifting techniques, risk assessment, correct posture and team lift procedures to prevent musculoskeletal injuries.

⚠️

Hazard Awareness

Identifying and responding to workplace hazards including slips, trips, blocked exits and vehicle routes.

🦺

PPE Awareness

Selecting, inspecting and correctly wearing personal protective equipment for different workplace roles and tasks.

🔥

Fire Safety

Fire risk recognition, alarm procedures, safe evacuation routes, assembly points and extinguisher use.

🚛

Moving Vehicles & Equipment

Safety rules for working near forklifts and moving vehicles — blind spots, pedestrian routes and authorisation.
