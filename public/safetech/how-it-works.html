How SafeWork Works

From login to certificate
in a few clear steps.

SafeWork is designed to be straightforward for everyone involved — workers, supervisors and administrators each have a clearly defined path through the platform, from first login to final compliance report.

The Full Process

Eight steps from setup to compliance

The SafeWork training cycle covers every stage — from an administrator setting up user accounts on day one, through to exportable compliance reports for auditors and regulators.

1

Account Setup

An Administrator creates user accounts for every worker and supervisor in the organisation. Each account is assigned a role — Worker, Supervisor or Administrator — which determines what the user can see and do inside the platform. Workers receive a username and password to access SafeWork through the login page. No software download is required.

2

Training Assignment

A Supervisor selects which training modules each worker is required to complete. Modules can be assigned individually or to multiple workers at once. Supervisors can set a due date for each assignment, allowing teams to plan and prioritise training around operational schedules. Assigned modules appear immediately in the worker's dashboard.

3

Learning Content

When a worker opens an assigned module, they are taken through a series of concise learning slides covering the key health and safety concepts for that topic. The content is written in plain, accessible language — no jargon, no unnecessary length. Workers can read at their own pace and review slides before moving on. Only once they have read through the content can they proceed to the scenario stage.

4

Interactive Scenario

After completing the learning content, the worker is presented with a realistic workplace scenario relevant to the module topic. The scenario describes a situation they might genuinely encounter — a spill in an aisle, an incorrectly stored load, a fire alarm activation — and asks them to choose the correct course of action from a set of options. The scenario reinforces the learning content in a practical, decision-making context before the formal assessment begins.

5

Knowledge Assessment

The final stage of each module is a timed multiple-choice quiz. Workers answer a set of questions on the content and scenario they have just completed, against a visible countdown timer. The default pass mark is 70%, although Administrators can configure this for each module. The quiz must be completed in one session — if the timer expires, the attempt is submitted automatically with the answers provided so far.

6

Results & Certificate

As soon as the assessment is submitted, the worker's score is calculated and displayed on screen instantly. If the worker has met or exceeded the pass mark, a training certificate is generated automatically. The certificate includes a unique reference code, the worker's name, the module name, the score achieved, and the date of issue. Certificates can be downloaded and printed directly from the worker's profile at any time.

7

Supervisor Review

Supervisors can see the status of every worker's training in real time through their team dashboard. The dashboard shows which modules have been completed, which are in progress, which have been failed and which are still pending. Supervisors can drill into individual workers to view attempt history, scores and certificates. Failed assessments are highlighted so that supervisors can follow up and arrange support or a retake as appropriate.

8

Reporting & Compliance Export

SafeWork generates a full training report covering all workers, modules, scores, attempts and certificates across the organisation. Reports can be exported as a CSV file, providing a structured compliance evidence trail suitable for internal audits, regulatory inspections or external accreditation reviews. Administrators have access to the full organisation-level report at any time.

Failed Assessments

What happens if a worker fails?

SafeWork is designed to support learning, not just compliance. Failure is treated as a step in the process, not the end of it.

Immediate Feedback

🔍 Score Displayed Instantly

When a worker submits an assessment that does not meet the pass mark, their score is shown immediately. The result is recorded against their profile so that supervisors and administrators can see the outcome in real time. There is no delay or manual review required before the worker is notified of their result.

Retake Policy

🔄 Retakes Are Permitted

Workers who fail an assessment are able to retake the module. Before retaking, they are encouraged to review the learning content and scenario again. This approach gives workers the opportunity to consolidate their understanding rather than simply re-sitting the quiz. The number of retakes a worker has attempted is tracked and visible to both the supervisor and the administrator.

Attempt Tracking

📊 All Attempts Are Recorded

Every assessment attempt — pass or fail — is recorded in the worker's training history with the date, score and outcome. Supervisors can see the full attempt log for each worker on each module, making it straightforward to identify workers who may need additional support. This complete record is also available in the compliance export for audit purposes.

Administrator Control

Full control over content, users and settings

Administrators have complete control over every element of the SafeWork platform — users, modules, questions and organisation-wide reporting — all from a single management interface.

User Management

👥 Manage All Users

Administrators can create new user accounts, update existing account details, reset passwords and change user roles at any time. If a worker changes role — for example, moving from operative to team leader — their SafeWork role can be updated to Supervisor without needing to create a new account. Users can also be deactivated without deleting their training history.

Module Management

📚 Manage Training Modules

Administrators can view and manage the full library of training modules available on the platform. Each module's content, scenario and pass mark can be reviewed and updated through the administration interface. New modules can be added to the library as the organisation's training requirements evolve, ensuring the platform grows with the business.

Question Management

❓ Manage Assessment Questions

The question bank for each module is managed by administrators. Questions can be added, edited or removed at any time. Administrators can also configure the number of questions presented in each assessment and adjust the pass mark — for example, increasing the required score for higher-risk modules to reflect the criticality of that training area.

Reporting

📈 Organisation-Wide Reports

Administrators have access to a full organisation-level compliance report showing every worker's training status across all modules. Reports include completion rates, pass rates, average scores, certificate counts and failed attempt summaries. The full report can be exported as a CSV file at any time, providing a structured and auditable record of the organisation's H&S training activity.
