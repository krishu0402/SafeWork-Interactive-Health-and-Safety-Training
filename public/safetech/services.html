What We Offer

Health & safety technology
for modern workplaces.

SafeTech Solutions delivers a complete workplace health and safety training platform — covering interactive e-learning, compliance tracking, reporting, and content management.

Core Service

Interactive H&S Training Platform

SafeWork is a fully browser-based health and safety training platform. Workers access their assigned training on any device — no installation required. Each module guides the worker through structured learning content, a realistic workplace scenario, and a knowledge assessment.

The platform is designed for warehouse, logistics and operational environments where training needs to be practical, accessible and trackable.

  • Five core H&S training modules included
  • Interactive scenario-based decision exercises
  • Timed knowledge assessments with configurable pass marks
  • Instant results with per-question explanatory feedback
  • Digital certificate generation on successful completion
  • Retake support with attempt count tracking
  • Works on desktop, tablet and mobile

For Supervisors & Administrators

Compliance Reporting & Monitoring

Real-time visibility of training progress, compliance status and team performance — all drawn live from the platform database.

Supervisor Dashboard

Team overview at a glance

Supervisors see live statistics across their team: total employees, completed training, in-progress modules, overdue assignments and overall compliance rate. Visual charts show module completion rates and status breakdown.

Training Reports

Detailed filtered reporting

The full training report shows every assignment across all workers, filterable by status (Passed, Failed, In Progress, Not Started, Overdue). Each record shows employee name, department, module, score, attempts and dates.

CSV Export

Exportable compliance records

Reports can be exported to CSV for use in external compliance documentation, audit trails or HR systems. Workers can also download their personal results as CSV from their certificates view.

Failed Assessments

Track who needs support

A dedicated failed assessments view shows supervisors exactly which workers have not yet passed each module, with their score, attempt count and due date — enabling targeted follow-up and support.

Admin Reports

Organisation-wide overview

Administrators access a system-wide dashboard showing total users by role, active modules, certificates issued and overall compliance percentage across all assignments in the organisation.

Certificate Records

Verifiable completion records

Every passed assignment generates a certificate with a unique reference code, the worker's name, module title, score achieved and date issued. Administrators can view all certificates issued across the platform.

For Administrators

Training Content Management

Administrators have full control over training modules, quiz questions and platform content — without needing technical knowledge.

Module Management

Create and edit training modules

Administrators can create new training modules with a title, description, duration and configurable pass mark. Existing modules can be edited or archived. Archived modules no longer appear in assignment lists but data is preserved.

Question Management

Build and edit quiz questions

Each module's knowledge assessment is built from questions managed in the admin panel. Administrators can add new questions with up to four answer options, mark the correct answer, add explanatory feedback text, and delete questions when needed.

User Management

Manage worker and supervisor accounts

Administrators can create new user accounts for workers and supervisors, edit existing user details, reset passwords and activate or deactivate accounts. Role assignment (Worker, Supervisor, Administrator) is controlled at account level.

Assignment Control

Supervisors assign training

Supervisors assign specific training modules to workers with a due date. The platform prevents duplicate assignments (same worker, same module) and records which supervisor assigned each piece of training for full audit traceability.

Technology

Platform architecture

SafeWork is built on a Node.js/Express backend with a SQLite database, served as a responsive browser-based application. The frontend uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frontend framework dependencies. Session-based authentication with bcrypt password hashing. Role-based route protection on all API endpoints. The platform runs on any standard web server environment.
