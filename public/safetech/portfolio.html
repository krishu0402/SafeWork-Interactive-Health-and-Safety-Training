SafeWork — Interactive H&S Training Platform

Internal product · Web application · Ongoing development

SafeWork is SafeTech Solutions' flagship product: a browser-based health and safety training platform designed for warehouse, logistics and operational teams. It provides an end-to-end training workflow with role-based access for workers, supervisors and administrators.

The platform supports five core training modules, each with interactive content slides, realistic workplace scenarios and knowledge assessments. Workers receive instant results, and passing workers are issued verifiable digital certificates. Supervisors can assign training, monitor team progress in real time and run filtered reports. Administrators manage users, training modules and quiz questions from a dedicated control panel.

SafeWork Training Module Library

Content development · Scenario design · Assessment authoring

Alongside the SafeWork platform itself, SafeTech Solutions develops the training content that powers each module. Each module is grounded in practical workplace situations relevant to operational environments and includes scenario-based decision exercises and knowledge assessments with explanatory feedback.

The module library currently covers five key areas of workplace health and safety, with questions written to reflect the everyday decisions workers face rather than abstract regulatory knowledge.

Compliance Reporting & Analytics Module

Dashboard development · Data visualisation · CSV reporting

Integrated within the SafeWork platform, the compliance reporting module provides supervisors and administrators with real-time visibility of training progress across their teams. The reporting system includes module-level completion rates, failed assessment tracking, overdue training flags, and full organisation-wide reports.

Reports can be filtered by status and exported to CSV for use in external compliance documentation. All statistics are drawn live from the database — no static or placeholder data.
