ABOUT SAFEWORK

Safety knowledge that works in the real world.

SafeWork supports warehouse, logistics and operational teams with practical learning that is clear, accessible and designed around everyday workplace decisions.

01

Our purpose

Make essential safety knowledge easier to access, understand and apply across a busy working day.

02

Our approach

Combine short learning content, scenario-based questions, feedback and progress records to reinforce safer habits.

03

Built for teams

Workers learn, supervisors support progress and administrators manage users, modules and reporting through separate access levels.

OUR COMMITMENT

Simple for workers. Useful for leaders.

The platform is designed to support consistent learning without making the experience complicated.

  • Clear learning journeys
  • Secure account-based access
  • Practical knowledge checks
  • Visible progress and reporting
  • Responsive access across devices
  • Support for continuous improvement
WORKERS

Build confidence

SUPERVISORS

Support the team

ADMINISTRATORS

Manage the platform
